03 The AI Copilot

Not a chatbot.
A morning brief
on the numbers that matter.

Every night, PlatePixel Copilot reads your day — POS sales, inventory levels, bookings, and customer history. By 7 AM, your phone has a one-page brief: what to prep, what to push, which regulars to call back, and where margin is leaking.

Demand forecasting

Predicts tomorrow's covers and tomorrow's prep, by station, by hour. Targeting 15–25% wastage reduction in the first quarter (early beta data).

SAMPLE BRIEF
Sat brunch: prep +18% benedicts. Storm forecast — pull -22% on outdoor seating.

Menu engineering

Classifies every dish by margin × velocity. Tells you what to push, what to re-price, what to retire — quietly.

SAMPLE BRIEF
Truffle pasta: high margin, low orders — feature it on the homepage hero this week.

Marketing automation

Drafts your captions, your WhatsApp campaigns, your Google reviews replies. In your tone, on your schedule, hands-off.

SAMPLE BRIEF
Drafted 3 reels captions and a WhatsApp blast for the new tasting menu — review by 11am.

Customer retention

Spots regulars going quiet and the ones about to churn. Reaches out before they forget — in your voice, on the right channel.

09 Questions

How is this different from buying a website + a POS + an ERP separately?
Three different vendors, three different login screens, three sets of data that don't talk. PlatePixel runs every layer on one stack — your website reads your live menu, your POS reads your inventory, your copilot reads everything. One brain, not three databases.
Do I have to take the whole stack?
No. Most businesses start with two or three layers — usually website + menu, sometimes plus brand. POS, ERP, and Copilot can come later, and they'll plug in without re-building the front. The curator above is honest about what you actually need today.
What does the AI Copilot actually do, in plain terms?
It reads your sales, inventory, calendar and customer data every night, and sends you a brief by 7am: what to prep, what to push, what's leaking margin, which customers to win back. It also drafts captions, WhatsApp campaigns, and reply-to-review messages in your tone. You approve; it sends.
Is the AI Copilot really live, or is this marketing copy?
Live, in private beta, with eight restaurants. We're being careful about scaling because the model has to learn your business before it's useful — usually 30–60 days. Public rollout: Q4 2026.
Who owns the data?
You do. Always. Your sales data, customer data, recipe data — yours to export at any time, in any format, no fees, no friction. PlatePixel never resells data, ever.
When does Retail launch?
Q3 2026. We're already building with three retail partners. If you're a boutique or speciality store interested in being an early stack, the curator above will route you to our retail team.
What happens after launch?
30 days of free care for studio layers, 90 days for full stacks. Then a small monthly care plan keeps the copilot trained, the platform updated, and a real human on call for the inevitable Saturday-night problem.
