01 Why PlatePixel

Built where small businesses
get left behind.

S
Sumanth · Co-founder
SV
Sushanth Varma · Co-founder

We grew up watching small businesses around us — restaurants, salons, neighbourhood stores — pay enterprise prices for stitched-together software that doesn't talk to itself.

Three vendors for the website. Another for the POS. A spreadsheet for inventory. WhatsApp screenshots for customer follow-ups. Nothing learns anything from anything else.

Meanwhile every Fortune 500 has an AI team turning the same kind of data — sales, inventory, customer history — into a real operating advantage. Small businesses don't.

PlatePixel is our answer to that gap. We design the brand. We build the digital stack — site, catalog, POS, ERP. And we integrate AI into the workflows that already run the business, so the café in Hyderabad gets the same operating leverage as the chain on the cover of a magazine.

We're in early pilot. The Ozone Salon POS in our portfolio is one of the first systems we've shipped end-to-end. More are in flight. If your business deserves a sharper brain — talk to us.

— Sumanth & Sushanth Varma · Co-founders, PlatePixel · Hyderabad
