01 AI Enablement

Not one product.
AI woven
through the work.

We don't sell one packaged AI copilot. We bring AI into the workflows you already run — Claude / GPT inside customer ops, WhatsApp automation, analytics that turn POS data into decisions, and custom AI tools shaped to your business. Four examples below.

Demand forecasting

Predicts tomorrow's bookings, covers, walk-ins, and prep — by station, by hour, by service. Targeting 15–25% wastage / idle-time reduction in the first quarter (early pilot data).

SAMPLE BRIEF
Sat brunch: prep +18%. Salon: 2 stylists idle 4–6pm — push WhatsApp slot offer.

Catalog & price intelligence

Classifies every dish, service, or product by margin × velocity. Tells you what to push, what to re-price, what to retire — quietly.

SAMPLE BRIEF
Hair botox: high margin, low bookings — feature it on the homepage hero this week.

Marketing & comms automation

Drafts your captions, your WhatsApp campaigns, your Google review replies, your post-visit follow-ups. In your tone, on your schedule, hands-off.

SAMPLE BRIEF
Drafted 3 reels captions and a WhatsApp blast for this week's launch — review by 11am.

Customer retention

Spots regulars going quiet and the ones about to churn. Reaches out before they forget — in your voice, on the right channel.

SAMPLE BRIEF
11 regulars haven't visited in 6 weeks. WhatsApp drafted — Mira's birthday is Thursday.
